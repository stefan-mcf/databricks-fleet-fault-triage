# Scope, limitations and research stopping point

This portfolio snapshot closes the current packaging phase. The research can be resumed later, but unfinished work is not relabelled as acceptance. The classifier's historical benchmark and Databricks workflow evidence remain separate from the supporting assistant's release decision.

## What the evidence supports

| Area | Recorded evidence | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classifier quality | One-use external benchmark, scored-record metrics and coverage | Retrospective recorded-fault classification; no field generalisation claim |
| Databricks execution | Completed jobs, MLflow/run readback, UC version 1 `READY`, parity | Single-node scikit-learn; no distributed training or serving claim |
| Delta scoring | Persisted 119 scores plus one abstention and payload readback | Training-record replay; not another quality test |
| Governance | Bounded model ownership, role and allowed/denied readback checks | No enterprise IAM or security-certification claim |
| Local controls | Synthetic contract, review, rejection and lifecycle checks | No genuine human acceptance or native promotion/rollback credit |
| Preservation | Exact model export and private hash/recovery checks | Weights and underlying private artifacts are not publicly inspectable |

The [evidence extracts and provenance](../evidence/provenance.json) identify their source and transformation. Public aggregate summaries are useful inspection material, but they do not make private native receipts independently reproducible. Hashes detect changes to an identified artifact; they do not prove an unavailable artifact's contents.

## Model validity

- `neg` denotes another recorded component fault, not a healthy vehicle. There is no healthy-fleet denominator.
- Vehicle identities, operating periods and measurement timing relative to failure are not established. Exact-duplicate isolation cannot remove unknown vehicle/time dependence or selection effects.
- Predictors are anonymised. Feature associations are not causal explanations or repair instructions.
- The 128 input-quality abstentions remain visible in coverage. Scored-record recall and precision cannot be extended to unscored inputs.
- Precision of 52.74% means the review burden is material: 310 of 656 flagged, scored records carried another component-fault label.
- Benchmark error weights are comparison units. No real repair-cost model, measured savings, operational ROI or advance-warning horizon is established.
- The original comparison did not retain every per-fold score. Recovered configuration means and recipe reproduction do not recreate those missing records.
- The external test was consumed once. Packaging does not rerun it, fit a new classifier or tune against its results.

See the [evaluation method](methodology.md) for the frozen recipe, denominators and dataset notices.

## Databricks boundaries

The workload demonstrates scikit-learn execution with MLflow and Unity Catalog on Databricks, plus Spark/Delta persistence. It does not demonstrate a feature store, a bronze/silver/gold medallion pipeline, distributed estimator training, managed model serving or production tenure.

Logged Delta datasource read-version metadata is not proof of explicit time travel. Native champion promotion, rejection and rollback acceptance remain incomplete. Local synthetic lifecycle demonstrations do not fill that gap. Historical job termination observations also do not establish the current state of cloud resources; this package makes no claim of completed infrastructure teardown or final invoice reconciliation.

## Supporting assistant: a rejected release candidate

The assistant adds bounded retrieval and investigation-brief assembly around the classifier. The latest hosted development candidate completed 40 saved outcomes per retrieval method. Two independent AI inventories rejected both methods on critical required-response completeness failures:

| Complete inventory | Keyword completion | Hybrid completion | Critical failures, keyword / hybrid |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 18/25 answerable (72%) | 18/25 answerable (72%) | 9 / 8 |
| B | 18/25 answerable (72%) | 18/25 answerable (72%) | 8 / 8 |

These are distinct inventories; their differing judgments and claim counts are not pooled or averaged. Supported facts, citations and exact numeric identities did not compensate for an incomplete required response. This was a failed release decision, not a successful assistant acceptance result. The [assistant status extract](../evidence/assistant-status.json) preserves the distinction.

Later engineering work did not complete hosted evaluation. Current public CI covers this curated package's tests and synthetic controls; it does not establish hosted assistant quality.

No current human output acceptance, retained SEALED-suite acceptance, supplemental judge completion or final native lifecycle acceptance follows. AI technical review is not human review. Controlled retrieval payloads that were not exposed to generation also establish no prompt-injection-resistance result.

## Reopening the research

A future continuation would need new, separately scoped execution authority and fresh provider/financial checks. Quality work must preserve frozen classifier results, consumed evaluation material and all failed assessments. It would require an eligible candidate's complete development review before any untouched held-out material, genuine human disposition/output acceptance, and separate native lifecycle proof.

These are future requirements, not remaining promises in the public demo. The current repository is ready to inspect as a bounded research portfolio snapshot. It supplies no authority to diagnose a real vehicle, issue maintenance instructions or return equipment to service, and implies no employer, donor or platform-vendor endorsement.
